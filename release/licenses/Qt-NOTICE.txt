The one-folder distribution leaves Qt libraries dynamically linked and replaceable.
In an AppImage, extract it first; libraries reside under
usr/lib/minecraft-playden-packager/_internal/PySide6/Qt/lib.
On Windows they reside under _internal/PySide6 alongside the Qt plugins.
Use the approved source revision and the published build procedure to rebuild.
